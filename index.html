Module 2: AI Essay Evaluator (Task 2)

Automated evaluation against the official IELTS Writing Band Descriptors.

1. Task Response

Checks if you answered all parts of the prompt, maintained a clear position, and supported your main ideas.

2. Coherence & Cohesion

Evaluates paragraph structure, logical flow, progression, and effective use of transitional phrases.

3. Lexical Resource

Scans vocabulary complexity, academic word choice, collocations, and spelling accuracy.

4. Grammar Range

Analyzes complex sentence structures, clause variation, punctuation, and grammatical error rates.
